## 3. Pricing model

GetResponse účtuje podle **počtu kontaktů** (active list size), ne podle počtu odeslaných emailů.

### 3.1 Co se počítá do kontaktů

- ✅ **Active subscribers** (opt-in)
- ❌ **Unsubscribed** (after opt-out) – NEpočítají se
- ❌ **Bounced contacts** (hard bounced) – NEpočítají se
- ❌ **Suppressed** – NEpočítají se

Pozn.: na rozdíl od Klaviya v 2026, kde se subscribed inactives počítají, GetResponse má **podobný model jako Brevo** – jen aktivně oslovené subscribers.

### 3.2 Unlimited emails – velký USP

- **Všechny placené plány mají unlimited emails**
- Toto je v industry rare (Mailchimp/Klaviyo limitují)
- Velmi výhodné pro **frequent senders** (daily newsletters, daily promos)
- Jednou platíte za list size, kolik posíláte je vaše věc

### 3.3 Tier upgrade

- Auto-upgrade pokud contacts cross threshold
- **Předem upozornění** před billing change
- Manual downgrade dostupný

### 3.4 List cleanup nemá takový billing impact

- Unsubscribed jsou v "Removed" stavu, nezapočítávají se
- Soft bounces po opakovaných pokusech přesunuty do "Removed"
- Hard bounces auto-cleaned

## 4. Lists, Contacts, Segmentation

### 4.1 Lists

- **Multiple lists per account**
- Contact lze v multiple lists současně (s billing implications)
- Per-list settings (default from, confirmation, opt-in method)
- **List import:** CSV, Excel, copy-paste, API, integrations
- **Import validation:** check for bounces, syntax errors

### 4.2 Contact data model

- **Standard attributes:** email, name (first/last), phone, company, address, IP, country, time zone, language
- **Custom fields** – unlimited, types: text, number, date, dropdown, multiple choice
- **Tags** – flat tag system
- **Lead score** – numeric score per contact
- **Custom score** properties
- **Activity timeline** per contact

### 4.3 Tags

- **Flat tag system** (no hierarchy)
- Add via: manual, automation action, import, API
- Multiple tags per contact
- Use jako trigger nebo segment filter
- Best practice: naming convention (e.g. "source_webinar_jan2026")

### 4.4 Custom Fields

- Per list nebo account-wide
- Use cases:
  - "Job Title"
  - "Industry"
  - "Birthday" (for date-based automations)
  - "Last Purchase"
  - "VIP status"
- **Merge field syntax** v emailech: `[[field_name]]`

### 4.5 Segments

- **Dynamic segments** (auto-update)
- Filter criteria:
  - Contact attributes (custom fields)
  - List membership
  - Engagement (opened/clicked specific campaign)
  - Behavioral (visited URL, custom events)
  - E-commerce (purchased, AOV, cart value)
  - Lead score
  - Tag membership
  - Date-based (birthday, signup date)
  - Source (form, import, integration)
- **Logical operators:** AND, OR, NOT
- **Unlimited segments** (závisí na plánu)

### 4.6 Lead Scoring (Marketer+)

- Manual rule-based:
  - Positive criteria (+ points): email open, link click, form submit, page visit
  - Negative criteria (- points): unsubscribed, no activity X days, bounced
- **Visual in contact profile** – current score visible
- **Trigger flows by score threshold**
- **Custom score properties** for multiple parallel scores

### 4.7 GetResponse CRM (light)

- Manage contacts s tags, segments, custom fields
- **Deal stages** (light – ne sales pipeline jako HubSpot)
- **Activity tracking**
- **Contact notes**
- **Custom contact properties**
- Not full CRM ale dostatečné pro většinu SMB

### 4.8 Consent management (GDPR)

- **Consent fields** – per consent type (newsletter, promo, transactional)
- Each contact má per-consent timestamp + IP audit trail
- Workflow filter for consent status

## 6. Marketing Automation Workflows

**Klíčový feature** od Marketer plánu nahoru.

### 6.1 Architecture

Workflow = sekvence **Conditions, Actions, Filters**.

- **Condition** = "X happened" (trigger or in-workflow check)
- **Action** = "Do Y" (when condition met)
- **Filter** = "For whom Y happens" (segment criteria)

### 6.2 Workflow types

#### A) From scratch
- Visual canvas drag-drop editor
- Plně customizable
- Stack conditions, actions, filters

#### B) From pre-built template
Categories:
- **Abandoned cart**
- **Affiliate marketing**
- **Engagement and retention**
- **Lead qualifying**
- **Online courses**
- **Post-purchase**
- **Sales promotions**
- **Webinars and events**

#### C) Imported workflows
- **Export/import workflows** (My templates tab) – jedinečné!
- Šíření know-how mezi účty

### 6.3 Conditions (full list)

#### Starting conditions (workflow entry)
- **Subscribed via form** – nová subscription
- **Subscribed via list import**
- **Subscribed via API**
- **Date-based** – birthday, custom date field
- **Tag added/removed**
- **Custom event** (web tracking)
- **Page visited** (URL tracking)
- **Course enrolled / lesson completed** (Content Monetization)
- **Webinar registration / attendance**

#### In-workflow conditions
- **Message opened** – specific email
- **Link clicked** – specific link
- **Subscription** – status check
- **Score reached** – lead score threshold
- **Custom field updated**
- **Tag check** – has/hasn't tag

#### E-commerce conditions (Ecommerce+)
- **Purchase made**
- **Specific product purchased**
- **Cart abandoned** – cart with specific products
- **Browse abandonment** – viewed product without purchase
- **Order value > threshold**

### 6.4 Actions (full list)

#### Communication
- **Send message** (email)
- **Send SMS** (MAX+)
- **Send push notification**

#### Contact management
- **Move to another list**
- **Copy to another list**
- **Remove from list**
- **Remove from account**
- **Add/Remove tag**
- **Update field** (set value)

#### Scoring
- **Score** – add/subtract points
- **Set score** (overwrite)

#### Workflow control
- **Wait** – fixed delay (with optional Time Travel for local time)
- **Wait until event** – behavior-based wait
- **Run multiple times** option for repeat actions

#### Webhooks & API
- **Webhook** – call external URL
- **Custom API call**

### 6.5 Filters (segment narrowing)

Place within workflow to narrow target:
- **Amount** – limit by first N contacts (great for VIP rewards, contests)
- **Range** – percentage split
- **Lists** – filter by current list membership
- **Tag** – filter by tag presence
- **Custom field value**
- **Consent** – filter by consent status

### 6.6 Workflow logic

- **Yes/No connectors** – conditional branching
- **Visual flow representation**
- **Real-time evaluation**
- **Workflow goal** – conversion tracking
- **Exit conditions** – remove from workflow when met

### 6.7 Web tracking integration

## 7. Autoresponders (legacy)

**Autoresponders** = legacy systém. **Existuje paralelně** s Marketing Automation Workflows.

### 7.1 Co jsou Autoresponders

- **Time/date-based drip emails**
- Single trigger: subscription to list
- Sequential delivery: Day 0, Day 3, Day 7, Day 14 atd.
- **No branching** – pure linear sequence
- **Simpler to set up** než workflows
- Available on **all plans including Free**

### 7.2 Kdy použít vs. workflows

| | Autoresponders | Workflows |
|---|---|---|
| Setup | Simple | Visual canvas |
| Logic | Linear | Branching |
| Triggers | Subscription only | Any condition |
| Available | All plans | Marketer+ |
| Best for | Simple drip campaigns | Complex customer journeys |

### 7.3 Migration path

- Autoresponders can be **referenced from workflows** ("Send autoresponder X as part of workflow Y")
- Both run **in parallel** – activating workflow doesn't disable autoresponders
- Best practice: migrate complex autoresponders → workflows over time

### 7.4 Limity

- No conditional logic
- No filters
- No actions beyond "send email"
- **Will not be deprecated** – GetResponse continues to support both

## 21. Limity a nedostatky

### 21.1 Plán limity

- **Starter má jen 1 custom workflow** – Marketer+ unlimited
- **Webinars jen Marketer+** – ne Starter
- **SMS jen MAX+** – nepřístupné většině
- **Dedicated IP jen MAX2** – ne add-on
- **Multi-user Team add-on** drahý ($20 + $5/user)

### 21.2 Funkcionální limity

- **CRM je light** – ne plný sales CRM jako HubSpot
- **No deal pipelines** v tradičním smyslu
- **No predictive analytics** (CLV, churn) – ne tak silné jako Klaviyo
- **No advanced lead scoring** s AI (jen rule-based)
- **No native review collection** – externí tooly (Yotpo, Judge.me)
- **Customer Data Platform missing** – ne tak silný jako Klaviyo KDP

### 21.3 Webinars limitations

- **Browser-based** – nativně instalovaný klient chybí
- **Max 1 000 attendees** (MAX2) – pro velké eventy jako Zoom levnější
- **No simulive** with hosts (jen evergreen recordings)
- **Streaming quality** varies based on bandwidth

### 21.4 UI/UX

- **Některé templates dated** – design ne tak polished jako Klaviyo
- **Two automation paradigms** (Autoresponders + Workflows) – matoucí pro nové users
- **Complex menu structure** – features spread

### 21.5 Migration

- **No native flow export to other platforms**
- Templates: HTML export OK
- Contacts: CSV/API export
- **Historical event data not portable**

### 21.6 Specific issues

- **Některé integrace via Zapier only** – ne native
- **Webhook latency** – občas delayed
- **Auto-update tier** when contacts grow – frustrating bez warning v některých případech
- **Annual commitment** for best pricing – no monthly toggle for some plans

## 22. Shrnutí: Pro koho a proti komu

### GetResponse je dobrá volba pokud
- Jste **content creator** – webinars + courses + email v jednom
- Hledáte **all-in-one growth platform** (email + landing pages + webinars + funnels)
- Provozujete **online course / coaching business**
- Máte **frequent send** patterns – unlimited emails advantage
- **GDPR/EU compliance** je důležitý (Polish hosting + EU DNA)
- Pracujete v **češtině/slovenštině/polštině** – jediný velký hráč s těmito UI lokalizacemi
- Hledáte **levnější alternativu** k HubSpotu s podobnou funkcionalitou
- Provozujete **mid-market e-commerce** bez extreme requirements
- Jste **agentura** s multiple klienty (MAX)
- Cíl je **all-in-one** za rozumný budget

### GetResponse není dobrá volba pokud
- Jste **enterprise B2B sales-led organization** (HubSpot, Salesforce lepší)
- Provozujete **velký Shopify store** s deep e-commerce needs (Klaviyo silnější)
- Potřebujete **deepest marketing automation** (ActiveCampaign deeper)
- Hledáte **deepest predictive analytics** (Klaviyo silnější)
- Máte **velkou inactive list** – billed per contact penalizuje neefektivní cleanup
- Hlavní use case je **pure transactional email** (Postmark, SendGrid, Brevo lepší)
- Potřebujete **dedicated infrastructure** s low budget (MAX2 vyžaduje)
- Hledáte **enterprise-grade security** (HIPAA compliance) – limited
