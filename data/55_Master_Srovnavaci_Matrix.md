## 7. Red flags – na co dát pozor per platforma

### 7.1 Mailchimp ⚠️

- **Aggressive scaling pricing** (drahé pro velké DB)
- **Hidden costs** (overage, premium templates)
- **Free plan limited** (jen 500 kontaktů, omezené features)

### 7.2 HubSpot ⚠️

- **Velmi drahé na pokročilé tiers** (Marketing Pro $800+/mo)
- **Lock-in** (deep CRM integration)
- **Steep learning curve**

### 7.3 Brevo ⚠️

- **Lower deliverability historically** (zlepšeno)
- **WhatsApp limit** v některých regionech
- **AI features méně vyspělé**

### 7.4 Klaviyo ⚠️

- **DTC focus = nefit pro B2B**
- **Pricing rychle roste** s velikostí DB
- **Free plan limitovaný** (250 cont.)

### 7.5 ActiveCampaign ⚠️

- **Žádný free plan**
- **Learning curve** pro deep automation
- **Komplexnost** pro beginners

### 7.6 GetResponse ⚠️

- **AI features marketing-heavy**, méně mature
- **Tier upgrades** pro pokročilé features
- **Webinar features = potential lock-in**

### 7.7 MailerLite ⚠️

- **B2C focus** (B2B limited)
- **Méně advanced automation** než ActiveCampaign
- **Free plan má restrictions** (no automation triggers)

### 7.8 Constant Contact ⚠️

- **Free plan ZRUŠEN červen 2025!**
- **Auto-upgrade trap** (bez confirmation)
- **Hard to cancel** (phone-only)
- **Hidden costs** ($0.002/email overage, $10 inbox preview, $10 SMS)
- **SMS jen USA**
- **Slabší automation** za vyšší cenu

### 7.9 SAP Emarsys ⚠️

- **Enterprise-only**, ne pro SMB
- **Žádné public pricing**
- **6-12 měsíců implementation**
- **Konzultant required**

### 7.10 Salesforce MC ⚠️

- **Velmi vysoké TCO** (implementace $25K+ na start)
- **Multi-product complexity** (Engagement, Account Engagement, Personalization, Intelligence, Loyalty)
- **Premier Success Plan = +30% nákladů**
- **Per-event/usage overage charges**
- **Vendor lock-in**

### 7.11 Braze ⚠️

- **Enterprise pricing** (custom)
- **Mobile-first** (web/email secondary)
- **Implementation complex**
- **US-centric** (EU support omezeně)

### 7.12 GetResponse / MailerLite (PL/LT origin) ⚠️

- Viz výše

### 7.13 SARE ⚠️

- **PL-primary** (limited international UI)
- **Custom pricing** (no transparency)
- **Limited public marketing**

### 7.14 SALESmanago ⚠️

- **Komplexnost** pro non-tech teams
- **Custom pricing** (no transparency)
- **AI features evolving** rapidly

### 7.15 ExpertSender ⚠️

- **Sales-driven** custom pricing
